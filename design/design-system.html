Stock Report design system, version 2

Dark first, compact, built for staff who reorder stock once a week. The Stock page tells a story: what is at risk, why, and what to order. Numbers sit next to small charts so the trend is visible without reading a table.

What changed from version 1

  • Eleven columns became six. Sales speed, trend, days left and the order stages are now small charts.
  • No KPI tiles. A count would only repeat the table, so the headline sentence says it once.
  • One main badge per product, with the status color on the icon and the label in plain ink.
  • The only filled button on the page is the reorder.
  • Filters and drill-downs come last: the segmented control above the list, then a row opens the runway chart below.

Charts and what each one answers

The chart type follows the question. Marks are thin, bars are capped at 24px with 4px rounded ends, gaps are 2px, and the data is the loudest thing on screen. The sparklines here use illustrative earlier days; the last 7 days match the seed data.

Creating an order is a modal with plan cards

No separate page. The Order button opens this over the Stock page. The three options differ in one thing that matters, the days of stock you end up with, and the SOP suggestion is preselected.

Type and space

IBM Plex Sans only. Sizes 12, 14, 16 and 20. Weights 400, 500 and 600. Spacing steps of 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40 and 48 px. Corners 6px on controls, 10px on panels, pills on badges.

Rules

Do

  • Start each screen with the decision and one sentence of takeaway.
  • Rank by urgency and keep the action on the right.
  • Use a chart when the trend or the threshold matters, a number when it does not.
  • Put create flows in modals and secondary pages in popovers.
  • Add filters and drill-downs after the story reads clearly.

Don't

  • Add KPI tiles that repeat a table count.
  • Use pie, donut, gauge, dual-axis or smoothed lines.
  • Color text with a series or status color.
  • Use gradients, glow, glass, skew or emoji on data screens.
  • Fill more than one button per view.

Motion: press scale 0.98 over 120ms, modal 160ms, only transform and opacity, nothing on hover or on anything staff do many times a day. Reduced motion removes the movement.
