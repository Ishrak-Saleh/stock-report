Words every screen uses the same way

Designing five pages together turned up places where the same word could mean two things. Fixing it here costs a sentence. Fixing it after coding would cost a bug.

Open order
Not cancelled, not closed, and units still to come. Arrivals match only open orders. The Stock page counts only open orders. The Chase list checks only open orders.
Unmatched arrival
Units that no open order could take. They still raise warehouse stock. They wait on the Arrivals page until someone matches them or marks them "not from an order".
Not from an order
A decision, not a gap. Units were counted into stock and a person said they belong to no order. Shown gray on Arrivals and named in history.
Today
The store's date in America/Denver, passed into every domain function. No screen reads the clock itself.
Who
The signed-in person's name. Order history, stock movements and the Outbox all use it. Imports show as Import.
Expected arrival
Order date plus lead time. An assumption, because the SOP has no date field. The runway chart and the design note both say so.
